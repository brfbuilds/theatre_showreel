# Theatre Showreel

Benjamin Fowler, actor: a 3D cinema website. Walk down an old cinema hallway past posters of my work. At the end, take your seat for a countdown, then the showreel or action reel.

Live at https://brfbuilds.github.io/theatre_showreel/

- `index.html`: the page, including the simple (non-3D) version.
- `assets/app.js`: the 3D scene (three.js).
- The reels stream from YouTube (IDs in `window.REELS` in index.html).
